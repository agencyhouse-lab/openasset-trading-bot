## ✅ Content Best Practices

### DO:
✅ Post real trades with real P&L
✅ Show losses too, not just wins
✅ Explain WHY something worked
✅ Keep posts short (mobile-first)
✅ Use emojis for clarity
✅ Link to /help and bot commands
✅ Respond to questions in group
✅ Post consistent schedule
✅ Update channel consistently (5-7x daily)

### DON'T:
❌ Make unrealistic claims (70%+ monthly)
❌ Hide losses or underperforming bots
❌ Use overhyped language
❌ Post every 10 minutes (spam)
❌ Ignore user questions
❌ Change fees without notice
❌ Make it feel like an MLM
❌ Sell separate products in channel
❌ Make financial advice claims

---

## 🎯 Tone Guide

Your channel should feel like:
- **Confident but humble** (we're good, but not perfect)
- **Transparent** (show both wins and losses)
- **Educational** (help people learn)
- **Professional** (clean formatting, no spam)
- **Community-focused** (celebrate users, ask for feedback)

NOT like:
- ❌ Get-rich-quick scheme
- ❌ Overhyped Tesla/Elon vibes
- ❌ Crypto bro culture
- ❌ Financial advice (you're not licensed)
- ❌ Secretive or mysterious
